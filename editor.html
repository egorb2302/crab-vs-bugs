    HOW IT WORKS

    Pick a tile and paint it on the map; right-click (or the sky tile) erases. Ctrl+Z undoes. One screen is 20×12 tiles; a level is a few screens joined left to right.

    The crab clears 3 tiles up or 4 across in one jump, never both. The check below treats anything past 2 up / 3 across as unreachable, so a level it passes can be beaten. The green tint shows every tile the crab can stand on.

    Bugs walk up to 3 tiles each way and turn at ledges, walls and hazards. A rail makes a 2-tile plank shuttle along it: draw - across or | down, at least 2 tiles long.

    PLAY tests the level right away. SHARE LINK needs a level with no errors: the whole map rides inside the link, so nothing is uploaded and there is no account. Your draft stays in this browser.
